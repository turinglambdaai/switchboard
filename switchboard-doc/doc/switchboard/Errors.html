6 Errors🔗ℹ

exn:fail:switchboard

└── exn:fail:switchboard:api          (fields: status body)

    ├── exn:fail:switchboard:auth             401, 403

    ├── exn:fail:switchboard:rate-limit       429

    ├── exn:fail:switchboard:not-found        404

    └── exn:fail:switchboard:context-window   400 blaming the context

Every provider failure crosses this boundary as one of these subtypes, so callers dispatch on structure instead of parsing provider error strings. A 400 whose body blames the context window is its own subtype because the recovery (trim the conversation and retry) differs from every other 400.
