7 The transport boundary🔗ℹ

(current-post-json) -> (-> string? (hash/c symbol? string?) jsexpr? jsexpr?)

All traffic flows through this parameter. Tests stub it and never touch the network; local gateways swap in at one place. Switchboard performs no hidden retries — retry and backoff policy lives above the library, where it can be owned and observed.
